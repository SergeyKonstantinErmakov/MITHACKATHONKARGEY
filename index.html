About Us

We chose to tackle the task “Anomaly Detection for Thermal Drone Footage and Visual Inspection". We were able to finetune a PaDiM model on the provided dataset using the library anomalib. This is a state of the art model for anomaly detection, which only requires the “normal” images in order to classify “anomalous” images. Team Member 1 (Karim) mainly worked on the fine-tuning aspect, trying to conquer the beast that is anomalib’s documentation, with hidden pages and links that point to nowhere. Team Member 2 (Sergey) mainly worked on the frontend and backend of the web interface, dedicating his time to making the app useable and responsive, and cleaning up Karim’s mess.
